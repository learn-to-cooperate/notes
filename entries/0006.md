# Cooperation vs Self-Reliance

**Date:** 2026-01-24

As the Italian proverb says, *“Chi fa da sé, fa per tre”* (literally “He who does things by himself does the work of three,” often translated as *“If you want something done right, do it yourself”*). The saying is often used to praise independence, but it also hints at a failure in cooperative settings: when coordination is missing, working together becomes so inefficient that acting alone seems more effective.

## Making Cooperation Work: Why Structure Matters

True cooperation only works when roles, responsibilities, and communication are clear. When collaboration is poorly coordinated, responsibility becomes blurred, decisions are delayed, and everyone waits for everyone else. Instead of producing better results, cooperation turns into paralysis rather than progress.
